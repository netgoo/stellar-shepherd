Pillar 1 — Data Intelligence

WCEI 0.94+ across 50+ providers $320 cost per 10k leads

Best For

Agencies and outbound teams needing multi-provider enrichment at scale, ICP qualification automation, and dedup pipelines.

Not For

Teams with under 5k records/month where single-vendor enrichment suffices, or teams needing on-premise data residency.

Not For
