Privacy Operational Protocols

This protocol governs the telemetry data boundaries of Wenboom. 1. Zero‑Backend Localized Architecture

Wenboom operates strictly as a zero‑backend, pure static environment. We do not maintain server‑side user relational databases, and we do not capture, proxy, or cache your proprietary business configurations, automated schema matrices, or internal API access keys. 2. Telemetry and Performance Analytics

To accurately evaluate public traffic channels (such as incoming routing signals from Reddit or X threads), this platform deploys a single, lightweight instrumentation layer: Google Analytics (GA4). Your continued programmatic usage of this resource network establishes an explicit agreement to these technical parameters.
